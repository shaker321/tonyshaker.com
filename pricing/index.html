FAQ

Common questions

How does employer reimbursement or sponsorship work?

It usually works in one of two ways:

  • Personal L&D Stipend: You pay at the standard individual rate ($1,500 or $550/mo) and submit my itemized business invoice (labeled Leadership & Professional Development Coaching) for reimbursement.
  • Direct Company Sponsorship: Your manager, VP, or HR team funds the engagement directly starting at $3,000, which covers formal proposals, vendor/W-9 paperwork, and optional manager alignment calls.

Either way, I can send you a concise, role-focused overview to share with your manager, and the contents of our coaching conversations stay strictly confidential.

What happens during the free 25-minute consult?
We'll talk honestly about where you are in your career, what's feeling stuck or draining, and what you're aiming for. You'll walk away with a few practical next steps regardless of whether we work together. If coaching makes sense for your situation, I'll briefly explain what an engagement would look like—no pressure and no hard sell.
What happens after the first 3 months?
Three months is long enough to build real momentum without locking you into a six-month commitment upfront. After Month 3, many clients wrap up with a clear plan in motion; others switch to a flexible $500/month continuation (two 50-minute sessions per month plus email check-ins, cancel anytime) or book occasional single strategy sessions as new milestones come up.
Can I start with one session and upgrade later?
Yes. If you book a $300 Single Strategy Session and decide within 14 days that you want ongoing support, I credit the full $300 toward a 3-Month Core Coaching engagement ($1,200 remaining for the next 5 sessions plus email support).
Who do you typically work with?
Because of my background, many of my clients are in tech—from early-career engineers and career switchers breaking into the field to experienced ICs and managers. That said, the core work of clarifying what you want, advocating for yourself, setting boundaries, and navigating transitions isn't industry-specific. I've helped everyone from accountants and a future maritime engineer to a professional illustrator and a priest. If you're navigating workplace friction, recovering from burnout, evaluating a sabbatical, or preparing for a meaningful shift, we can likely work well together regardless of your field.
What is your rescheduling policy for paid sessions?
Life happens. Paid sessions can be rescheduled anytime with at least 24 hours' notice using your calendar link. Sessions canceled with less than 24 hours' notice (or missed without notice) count toward your package, with reasonable exceptions for emergencies.
Do I need to know what I want before we start?
Not at all. Most clients reach out because they know something feels off, not because they already have a five-year plan. Figuring out what you actually want—and separating your own goals from everyone else's expectations—is often the first part of our work together.
How is career coaching different from therapy?
Career coaching is forward-looking and action-oriented—focused on your decisions, positioning, boundaries, and next steps at work. It is not licensed therapy, counseling, or medical care, and it isn't a substitute for clinical mental health support. The two can complement each other well, and if it ever seems like a therapist would serve you better, I'll tell you honestly.
