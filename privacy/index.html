Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Tony Shaker LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates tonyshaker.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website, schedule a consultation, or contact us, how we use that information, and the choices you have.

1. Information You Provide Directly

We collect personal information only when you choose to share it with us—for example, when you book a consultation through Cal.com, send an email to hello@tonyshaker.com or tony@tonyshaker.com, or enroll as a coaching client. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and optional phone number
  • Scheduling preferences, time zone, and any career context or notes you provide
  • Billing and invoicing details if you purchase coaching services (processed through third-party payment providers; we do not store full credit card numbers on this site)

2. Website Analytics & Session Replay

We use two analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with this site and improve its clarity and usability:

  • Microsoft Clarity: We partner with Microsoft Clarity and Microsoft Advertising to capture how you use and interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve and market our services. Website usage data is captured using first- and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies to determine the popularity of services and online activity. Additionally, we use this information for site optimization and fraud/security purposes. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, visit the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Umami Cloud: We use Umami, a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics service, to measure aggregate page views, referring websites, button clicks, and general device/location trends without tracking you across other websites.

We also use browser sessionStorage temporarily during your visit solely to preserve campaign referral parameters (UTM tags) if you navigate between pages before booking a call.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and send reminders for consultations and coaching sessions
  • Respond to inquiries and deliver coaching services
  • Issue invoices and maintain standard business and tax records
  • Understand website performance and improve content and navigation

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

To run this site and deliver coaching smoothly, we rely on trusted third-party service providers that handle data under their own privacy policies, including:

  • Cal.com (appointment scheduling and automated email reminders)
  • Google Workspace / Google Meet (email correspondence, calendar invites, and video sessions)
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)
  • Payment processors such as Stripe (if you purchase paid coaching packages)

Links on this site to external websites (such as LinkedIn) are governed by those sites' own privacy practices.

5. Data Retention & Confidentiality

We keep your contact and scheduling information only as long as needed to communicate with you, deliver coaching services, or satisfy legal and accounting obligations. Confidentiality of paid coaching sessions is further governed by your signed Client Coaching Agreement.

6. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of analytics on this site by visiting tonyshaker.com/?no_track=1 (which sets a local opt-out preference in your browser). Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email hello@tonyshaker.com.

7. Children's Privacy

This website and our coaching services are intended for working adults and are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version. Questions about this policy can be sent to:

Tony Shaker LLC
Email: hello@tonyshaker.com
