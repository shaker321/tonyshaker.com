About Tony

Career coaching backed by experience.

Hi! I'm Tony. I've spent over a decade as a software engineer at Meta, Bloomberg, and early as well as established startups. I did all that as an Ivy League dropout who took one of the least traditional paths I've heard of. I left my first engineering job at a startup to direct and produce a movie in a 6-story boobytrapped abandoned hotel. I then went to work at Bloomberg for a few years and later a startup as their first engineer. I left that job and spent over a year driving 60,000 miles across the lower 48 states in my Subaru camping out on patches of desert and mountain tops, getting intentionally lost, and turning strangers into friends. I got back and started teaching Amazon warehouse associates how to become professional software developers while doing advisory work for early stage startups before landing a job doing Machine Learning Infrastructure at Meta. I spent a few years there before leaving to start my own career coaching practice.

So how does a college dropout, sometimes film director, and professional wanderer land jobs at Meta, Bloomberg, startups, and teaching roles?

By understanding how to market myself, communicate effectively, navigate office politics, set boundaries, push back when needed, and overall having the confidence to shape my own career the way I want it to look. I know my life is short. I don't want to waste it living someone's dream.

I want to help you do the same whether that's in your current role or your next one.
